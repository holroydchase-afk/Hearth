## 0. Bottom line

- **Hearth isn't venture-fundable yet, and that's fine.** It's a pre-traction consumer app with a solo founder. Pre-seed money in 2026 is going mostly to SaaS, hardware, biotech and AI (Carta). The closest comparables have either:
  - stayed bootstrapped (Ente),
  - been funded by a parent corporation (FamilyAlbum is owned by MIXI), or
  - struggled to raise in public markets: Tinybeans offered A$3.5M in Dec 2025 at A$0.10 per share and raised only about A$1.7M.
- **Over the next 90 days the money comes from three places:**
  1. Founding-family pre-sales, which are mostly a validation signal.
  2. The AARP AgeTech Collaborative, which offers an accelerator path rather than cash.
  3. Setting up the paperwork that unlocks South Carolina non-dilutive money in Q1 2027. The SCRA Startup Grant is worth up to $25K.
- **NIH SBIR is the only large non-dilutive pot that fits,** at $300K–$700K for Phase I. It's a 100–200 hour research proposal. Target the **Apr 5, 2027** or **Sep 2027** cycle with beta data, not Jan 5.
- **West Virginia and North Carolina money is mostly out of reach.** Those programs require you to be based there (WV registration or residency; NC headquarters plus an NC-resident founder). Tega Cay, SC is an advantage only for **South Carolina** programs.
- **YC and a16z speedrun are long shots,** and both require **about 3 months in person in San Francisco** (YC Jan–Mar 2027; speedrun Feb 1–Apr 16, 2027). With young kids at home, decide that first.

## 2. Revenue as funding: pre-sales and lifetime deals

**What the store rules allow (Apple's [App Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/), read Oct 15, 2026):**
- **2.2 Beta Testing:** "apps using TestFlight cannot be distributed to testers in exchange for compensation of any kind, including as a reward for crowd-sourced funding."
  - So the beta must stay free for everyone invited. A pre-sale can buy the *future* Family plan. It can't buy beta access.
  - Don't run a Kickstarter-style campaign whose reward is TestFlight access.
- **3.1.1(a) / 3.1.3, US storefront:** US apps may include "buttons, external links, or other calls to action" to web purchases without an entitlement. No Apple commission on US link-outs is published ([SECONDARY: Newly, Oct 10, 2026](https://newly.app/how-to/app-store-external-purchase-link)).
  - Outside the US, link-outs need an entitlement.
  - Selling on your own website to your own family, outside the app, is allowed: "Developers can send communications outside of the app."
- **3.1.3(b) Multiplatform:** a web-bought subscription can unlock features in the iOS app "provided those items are also available as in-app purchases within the app."
  - So when the paid plan ships on iOS, an IAP version must exist too.
  - Plan a non-consumable or auto-renew IAP equivalent for any founding offer.
- **Google Play (US):** since Oct 29, 2025, Google doesn't require Play Billing for US users ([Play Help](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/15582165?hl=en)).
  - Developers who link out must enroll in the External content links program.
  - Fee reporting starts Oct 1, 2026; the deadline for link-out reporting is Dec 1, 2026. Fee levels are still in litigation ([SECONDARY: Stash](https://www.stash.gg/blog/googles-lower-fees-announcement-june-2026-whats-really-going-on-and-what-us-developers-should-do)).
  - Simplest for v1: no link-out in the Android build. Sell on the web only.

**Recommended offer (my judgment, not a researched benchmark):**
- **"Founding Family": $39.99/yr, price-locked for life.** Optionally offer 2 years prepaid for $69. Charge when the Family plan's features ship, or charge now with a full refund on request.
- **Avoid an uncapped "lifetime" deal.** Storage compounds by about 1 GB per family per year (docs/eng/research.md §6). If you do one:
  - cap it at 100 families,
  - price it at ≥$149,
  - set a written fair-use video limit.
- **Gift angle:** "A grandparent pays for the family." This matches the $29.99/yr Gift plan in docs/eng/research.md. Launch it right after Thanksgiving.
- **Honest expectation:** a few hundred to a few thousand dollars. The real value is the **paid-conversion number**, which every investor and SCRA reviewer will ask for.

## 4. Paperwork: when each item matters

| Item | When it matters | Cost / time |
|---|---|---|
| **SC LLC** | **Now.** Needed for the Apple and Google org accounts (Apple: "DBAs … are not accepted"; a sole proprietor enrolls as an individual), for SCRA, and to keep pre-sale money out of personal accounts | $110 filing; ~1–2 business days online ([Harbor Compliance, SECONDARY](https://www.harborcompliance.com/south-carolina-articles-of-organization)). | **EIN** | Right after the LLC: bank account, Stripe, D-U-N-S | Free; issued immediately online ([IRS](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/get-an-employer-identification-number)) |
| **D-U-N-S** | Before Apple org enrollment; also for the Play org account | Free; up to 5 business days from D&B plus up to 2 for Apple ([Apple](https://developer.apple.com/help/account/membership/D-U-N-S/)) |
| **SAM.gov registration + UEI** | **Only if you pursue NIH SBIR** (plus Grants.gov and eRA Commons). The UEI replaced DUNS for federal awards on Apr 4, 2022 ([GSA](https://www.gsa.gov/about-us/organization/federal-acquisition-service/technology-transformation-services/integrated-award-environment-iae/iae-systems-information-kit/unique-entity-identifier-update)) | Free [sam.gov page blocked; UNVERIFIED]. Start ≥6–8 weeks before a deadline [EST] |
| **Delaware C-corp** | **Not yet.** Convert when you take priced equity, a SAFE from institutional investors, an accelerator deal, or plan Reg CF. YC: you don't need to incorporate before applying. SCRA accepts a DE C-corp registered in SC as a foreign entity | LLC→C-corp conversion cost [UNVERIFIED; typically a few hundred to low thousands with counsel]. SCRA grant money can't pay legal fees |

**Why an SC LLC now rather than a C-corp:** it's cheap and fast, satisfies Apple, Google and SCRA, and converts cleanly once an investor actually asks. The SBIR rules (US small business, ≤500 employees, US ownership) work with either.
