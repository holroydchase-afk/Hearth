# How to see the grandkids' photos

**You don't need a password. You don't need to download anything.**
**It's free, and it will never ask you for money.**

---

## Step 1. Look for a text from Chase

When there are new photos, your phone gets a text message like this one:

> **[SCREENSHOT 1: the text message on her phone, reading
> "From Chase on Hearth: 2 new photos of Mia. Tap to see them: hearth.family/..."]**

The text always starts with **"From Chase on Hearth."**
If a text does not start that way, don't tap it. Call Chase.

---

## Step 2. Tap the blue link in the text

The photos open on your phone. The newest ones are at the top.
Slide your finger up the screen to see more.

> **[SCREENSHOT 2: the photos page, with the first photo circled]**

---

## Step 3. Tap a photo to make it big

The photo fills the screen.

- To see the next photo, tap **Next**.
- To keep a photo, tap **Save to my phone**. It goes in your Gallery with your other pictures.
- To go back, tap **Close**.

> **[SCREENSHOT 3: one big photo, with "Save to my phone" circled]**

---
