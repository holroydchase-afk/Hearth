# Decision: notices name the sender for the beta (D9, BETA-NOTIFY-7)
Date: Oct 23, 2026 · Status: decided by Chase (power hour, "I agree with everything in 6") · Source: TRACKER D9

Push and email notices keep saying who sent the photo ("From Chase on Hearth"). A "hide names" setting comes later, after the beta.

| Persona | Green/Red | Why |
|---|---|---|
| GG Great-grandparent who won't install | Green | An email from a named grandchild gets opened, not deleted as spam |
| LG Least-savvy grandparent | Green | A known name tells her the notice is real and safe to tap |
| AG Android grandpa | Green | Same notice text on Android |
| SG Step or other-household grandparent | Red | A lock-screen name could show which household shared what |
| CU Cousin | Green | Knows at a glance whose photos are new |
| PA Posting parent | Green | Built today; nothing to change |
| FA Far-away inviter | Green | "Look for a notice from Chase" is an easy instruction |
| TR Tech reader | Red | A sender name on the lock screen leaks to anyone near the phone |

Resolution: SG mitigation: notices name only the sender and never the group or audience, so nothing about who else saw it shows.
Resolution: TR mitigation: notices carry no caption or photo; a "hide names" setting is logged for after the beta (TRACKER).
