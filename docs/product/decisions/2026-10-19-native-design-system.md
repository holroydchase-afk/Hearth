# Decision: Hearth follows Apple's HIG on iPhone and Material 3 on Android, with Hearth's stricter overrides
Date: Oct 19, 2026 · Status: proposed · Source: Chase, Oct 19: "can't we go 100% apple UI/UX. guidelines, guardrails, components, fonts, etc. and maybe the same for android". Spec: `docs/product/DESIGN-SYSTEM.md` (DS-1..DS-9)

| Persona | Green/Red | Why |
|---|---|---|
| PA Posting parent | Green | Looks like every other iPhone app; less to learn |
| FA Far-away inviter | Green | Familiar system screens are easier to describe over the phone |
| LG Least-savvy grandparent | Green | Dynamic Type works; overrides keep words under icons and no gesture-only actions |
| AG Android grandpa | Green | Native Android look, same features, photos and words as iPhone |
| SG Step or other-household grandparent | Green | No change to who sees what |
| GG Great-grandparent who won't install | Green | Web viewer gets the system font; email and frame unchanged |
| TR Tech reader | Green | Native components bring proper VoiceOver and TalkBack support |
