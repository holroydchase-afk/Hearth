# Decision: compress to 2048 px JPEG for M1; full size is one policy value
Date: Oct 19, 2026 · Status: Chase's · Source: Chase, Oct 19: "Im fine with compression right now, but why is full size or heic more work?" Spec: `docs/product/SPEC-MEDIA.md` D7

| Persona | Green/Red | Why |
|---|---|---|
| PA Posting parent | Green | Fast uploads now; full size later is a one-value change |
| FA Far-away inviter | Green | No effect on setup |
| LG Least-savvy grandparent | Green | Small files load on one bar of LTE (G2) |
| AG Android grandpa | Green | Same 2048 px JPEG on every phone (SHARE-5) |
| SG Step or other-household grandparent | Green | Same quality for every household; nobody gets the lesser copy |
| GG Great-grandparent who won't install | Green | 2048 px fills a frame and fits in email |
| TR Tech reader | Green | Re-encode is also what makes the strip checkable |

Note: flipping to full size later needs its own check; LG and AG likely go Red on slow signal.
