# Decision: photo helpers with no model (duplicates, screenshots, blurry photos) set photos aside in bulk sends
Date: Oct 21, 2026 · Status: specced (Chase, Oct 21: "could be specced now") · Source: `docs/product/SPEC-PHOTO-HELPERS.md`

| Persona | Green/Red | Why |
|---|---|---|
| GG Great-grandparent who won't install | Green | Fewer near-identical photos arrive by email |
| LG Least-savvy grandparent | Green | Never sees helpers; posters only. Fewer duplicates to scroll |
| AG Android grandpa | Green | Less data to download on an old phone |
| SG Step or other-household grandparent | Green | Same audience rules; only fewer photos |
| CU Cousin | Green | Bulk sends get shorter and cleaner |
| PA Posting parent | Red | A wrongly set-aside photo could be missed before sending |
| FA Far-away inviter | Green | Faster sends on weak signal |
| TR Tech reader | Green | Hashes and scores stay on the phone; no model |

Resolution: PA mitigation: every set-aside reason is a worded line with a one-tap "Send them too", plus "Show what we left out" with Put back on each photo (HELP-R1, HELP-ALL-1).
