What changed from today's card
  • A moment is one compact card: name, when and who it went to, then photos, then one row of reactions.
  • Several photos show as a collage with "+4", so you see the whole moment at once. Tap any photo to see it whole and swipe or use Back and Next.
  • Photos fill their space (no grey bars). Nothing is cut off in the full-screen view.
  • Love, Haha, Like and Comment sit in one row, each with its word under it.
  • Only the newest comment shows. Writing one opens its own screen that says who will read it.
  • Delete and "Who can see this" moved behind a worded More button.
  • "Seen by" is gone, as you decided. Older moments load as you scroll.
